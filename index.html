Frequently Asked Questions About Turnkey Flat Interiors

Straightforward answers from founder & master woodworker Akhlaque Chaudhary regarding interior budgets, material specifications, 710 BWP plywood, and project timelines in Moshi and Chikhali.

Q. How much does a turnkey 2BHK flat interior cost in Moshi and Chikhali PCMC?

Turnkey 2BHK interiors in Moshi and Chikhali by ANFA Interior range between ₹4.8 Lakh and ₹7.2 Lakh at direct workshop rates. This includes an L-shape or parallel modular kitchen in 100% IS 710 BWP Marine ply, two full wardrobes with lofts, living room TV unit, false ceiling with LED lights, and painting. Venture-backed design studios typically charge ₹6.5L to ₹9.5L for comparable specifications.

Q. Why choose a direct contractor like ANFA Interior over aggregators like Livspace or HomeLane?

Unlike aggregator studios that outsource execution to third-party sub-contractors and add a 30% to 40% sales commission, ANFA Interior executes all woodworking in our own Kudalwadi, Chikhali workshop under founder Akhlaque Chaudhary. Clients get 100% genuine BWP Marine plywood, custom millwork fitted to site beam offsets, and zero middleman markups.

Q. Which plywood grade is used for modular kitchens and wardrobes in PCMC homes?

ANFA Interior uses 100% IS 710 BWP (Boiling Water Proof) Marine Grade Plywood (Century / Greenply) for all kitchen carcasses, sink cabinets, and bathroom vanities to ensure zero water damage. For dry bedroom wardrobes, we use calibrated MR commercial ply or 710 marine ply, finished with 1mm high-pressure Merino laminates and Hettich soft-close hinges.

Q. How long does a complete turnkey flat interior project take from start to handover?

ANFA Interior guarantees a 45-day handover timeline for standard 1BHK and 2BHK flats in PCMC. Because all cutting, carcass assembly, and laminate pressing take place in our in-house workshop, on-site dust and noise are minimized. Site installation, electrical wiring, false ceiling, and painting typically take just 10 to 14 days after factory fabrication.

Q. Can I inspect timber, plywood brands, and hardware before installation?

Yes. We maintain 100% material transparency. Clients can inspect raw Century/Greenply boards, 1mm laminate sheets, and branded Hettich/Hafele hardware before cutting starts. Physical material catalogs and finish samples are also brought directly to your flat during the free site measurement visit across Moshi, Chikhali, and Tathawade.

Q. How do I get an exact room-wise BOQ and quotation for my flat?

You can use our instant Cost Estimator or contact Akhlaque Chaudhary directly at +91 70402 08012 for a free doorstep measurement. Within 24 hours of site inspection, we provide a transparent, itemized Bill of Quantities (BOQ) with exact running foot and square foot measurements and zero hidden charges.
