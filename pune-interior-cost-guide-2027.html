Protect Your Budget

3 Interior Cost Traps to Avoid in Pune in 2027

How to read any contractor or interior studio quotation before paying a booking token.

TRAP #01

Fake "Waterproof Marine Ply" Labels

Many low-quote contractors bill you for "Marine Ply" while sneaking IS:303 commercial ply or HDF/particle board into kitchen sink cabinets, which swell within 14 months. Always verify the physical IS:710 BWP red/blue manufacturer stamp before cutting.

TRAP #02

Running Foot vs. Square Foot Bait Pricing

Some studios advertise "₹999/sq.ft." wardrobes, then double-bill internal drawers, loft shutters, handles, transport, and edge-banding as hidden extras during final invoicing. Demand an all-inclusive itemized BOQ upfront.

TRAP #03

Aggregator Sub-Contractor Markups

Large online interior brands charge ₹7.5L–₹10L for a 2BHK, pocket 30–35% for showroom rent and sales commissions, and sub-contract execution to third-party carpenters. Working directly with our workshop saves ₹1.5L–₹3.0L with superior materials.
